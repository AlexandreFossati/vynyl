# product-management-api Specification

## Purpose

Define the HTTP contract for getting, creating, updating and removing a catalog product, completing the statement's required operations together with the listing.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Get a product
`GET /api/products/:id` SHALL respond `200` with the product of the same `id`, in the same representation used by the listing. If it does not exist, it SHALL respond `404` with the code `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`.

#### Scenario: Existing product
- **WHEN** a client calls `GET /api/products/1` and product 1 exists
- **THEN** the response is `200` with the product of `id` 1 identical to the corresponding item in the listing

#### Scenario: Nonexistent product
- **WHEN** a client calls `GET /api/products/9999` and that product does not exist
- **THEN** the response is `404` with `error.code` equal to `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`

### Requirement: Identifier validation
The `:id` segment SHALL be a positive decimal integer (digits only, value greater than zero). Any other value SHALL be rejected with `400` and `VALIDATION_ERROR`, identifying `id` in `details`, without querying the database.

#### Scenario: Invalid identifiers
- **WHEN** a client uses `abc`, `0`, `-1`, `1.5` or `1e2` as `:id` in `GET`, `PATCH` or `DELETE`
- **THEN** each request receives `400` with `error.code` equal to `VALIDATION_ERROR` and `details` pointing to `id`

### Requirement: Create a product
`POST /api/products` SHALL validate the JSON body with the product rules (title, description, category, price, stock, brand, SKU and weight) and respond `201` with the created product, including the server-generated `id` and `meta`, and the `Location` header with the new resource's path (`/api/products/<id>`). The `id` SHALL be generated by the database and `meta.createdAt` and `meta.updatedAt` SHALL receive the same current instant in ISO 8601 UTC format. The body SHALL NOT accept `id` or `meta`, nor any unknown field.

#### Scenario: Successful creation
- **WHEN** a client sends a valid body to `POST /api/products`
- **THEN** the response is `201`, the body contains the product with a new `id` (greater than the largest existing one) and filled `meta`, and `Location` points to `/api/products/<id>`

#### Scenario: Created product is retrievable
- **WHEN** the client does a `GET` on the path received in `Location`
- **THEN** the response is `200` with the same product returned on creation

#### Scenario: Price in exact cents
- **WHEN** a product is created with `price` equal to `19.99`
- **THEN** the returned product and the one retrieved afterwards have `price` equal to `19.99`

#### Scenario: Server-controlled fields
- **WHEN** the body includes `id` or `meta`
- **THEN** the response is `400` with `VALIDATION_ERROR` and no product is created

#### Scenario: Invalid or missing fields
- **WHEN** the body has a negative `price`, a `sku` outside the pattern, a missing required field or an unknown field
- **THEN** the response is `400` with `VALIDATION_ERROR` and `details` pointing to each problematic field

#### Scenario: Missing body or body that is not an object
- **WHEN** the body is empty, is an array or the `Content-Type` is not JSON
- **THEN** the response is `400` with `VALIDATION_ERROR`

### Requirement: Unique SKU
No pair of products SHALL share the same `sku`. Creating or updating a product with a `sku` already used by another product SHALL respond `409` with the code `SKU_CONFLICT`, without changing data. The guarantee SHALL come from the database's uniqueness constraint, so that concurrent requests do not create duplicates.

#### Scenario: Creation with an existing SKU
- **WHEN** a client creates a product with the `sku` of an existing product
- **THEN** the response is `409` with `error.code` equal to `SKU_CONFLICT` and the catalog keeps the same number of products

#### Scenario: Update to another product's SKU
- **WHEN** a client updates product A providing the `sku` of product B
- **THEN** the response is `409` with `SKU_CONFLICT` and product A remains unchanged

#### Scenario: Update keeping its own SKU
- **WHEN** a client updates a product providing the same `sku` it already has
- **THEN** the response is `200`

### Requirement: Partially update a product
`PATCH /api/products/:id` SHALL accept a JSON body with one or more of the product's fields (the same validation rules as creation, all optional, at least one required), change only the fields provided and respond `200` with the complete updated product. `meta.updatedAt` SHALL receive the current instant and `meta.createdAt` SHALL remain unchanged. The body SHALL NOT accept `id`, `meta` or unknown fields. A nonexistent `id` SHALL respond `404` `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`.

#### Scenario: Update of one field
- **WHEN** a client sends `PATCH /api/products/1` with `{ "stock": 7 }`
- **THEN** the response is `200`, `stock` is 7, the other fields stay the same, `meta.createdAt` does not change and `meta.updatedAt` is later than the previous one

#### Scenario: Empty body
- **WHEN** the body is `{}`
- **THEN** the response is `400` with `VALIDATION_ERROR`

#### Scenario: Disallowed or invalid fields
- **WHEN** the body includes `id`, `meta`, an unknown field or an invalid value (for example negative `stock`)
- **THEN** the response is `400` with `VALIDATION_ERROR` and the product is not changed

#### Scenario: Nonexistent product
- **WHEN** the client does a `PATCH` on an `id` that does not exist, with a valid body
- **THEN** the response is `404` with `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`

### Requirement: Remove a product
`DELETE /api/products/:id` SHALL permanently remove the product and respond `204` with no body. A nonexistent `id` (including one already removed) SHALL respond `404` `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`.

#### Scenario: Successful removal
- **WHEN** a client calls `DELETE /api/products/1` and the product exists
- **THEN** the response is `204` with no body and a later `GET` on that `id` responds `404`

#### Scenario: Repeated removal
- **WHEN** the client repeats the `DELETE` of the same `id`
- **THEN** the response is `404` with `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`

#### Scenario: Total reflected in the listing
- **WHEN** a product is created or removed
- **THEN** the `total` of `GET /api/products` increases or decreases by 1
